About me

I'm a Technical Artist and XR Developer based in Germany, recently graduated from Hochschule Darmstadt's Augmented and Virtual Reality Design programme.

My bachelor thesis built a generative AI pipeline that produces Unity shader graphs from natural language — bridging the gap between what artists can describe and what real-time engines can run.

I work mostly in Unity and C#, with a focus on shaders, real-time rendering, and tools that make creative work faster.
